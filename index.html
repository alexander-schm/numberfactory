Zahlenwerk

The number factory needs a dispatcher. Keep the tiles flowing!

  1. Numbered tiles pile up in the factory. Send the next one with Space or a click on the factory.
  2. Each mailbox wants a number. Its station changes every tile on the way in: a 7 going through +3 arrives as 10.
  3. Steer tiles with the switches: press 1–4 (or click a mailbox) to set the whole route, 0 for the recycler. You can also click a single switch.
  4. A switch only matters when a tile reaches it. Change the route after the previous tile has passed.

Orders that run out of time cost a gear ⚙. Lose all 5 and the factory stops. Each new shift gives a gear back.
If 6 tiles pile up, the factory jams and sends tiles out by itself. Wrong numbers are sent back (−25).
Tiles show where they're headed: ✓ fits, ✕ doesn't, ♻ recycler, 2 = another tile gets that order first. P pauses.
