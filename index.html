Zahlenwerk

The number factory needs a shift manager. Fill the orders!

  1. A numbered tile waits in the factory. It only leaves when you click the factory (or press Space).
  2. Click a station to change its operation: +, − or ×. Click a switch to change where tiles go.
  3. The dotted preview shows where the waiting tile will end up and what number it becomes. Send it when the preview ends on ✓.
  4. Each mailbox wants one exact number before its timer runs out. Every tile can fill at least one order.

An order that runs out of time costs a gear ⚙. Lose all 5 and the shift is over. Finishing a shift gives a gear back.
Wrong numbers get sent back (−25 points, combo lost).
Tiles already moving show ✓ / ✕ / ♻, so you can tell if changing a station would ruin their delivery. A “2” means another tile gets to that order first.
R or right-click the factory swaps the tile (−10 points) · P pauses
